First Principles
Muscle = longevity currencySkeletal muscle mass predicts survival in aging more than almost any other biomarker. Build and preserve it now.
VO₂max is the strongest predictor of all-cause mortalityImproving aerobic capacity from "low" to "above average" cuts risk of death in half. It is trainable at any age.
Sleep is not optional recovery — it is mandatory maintenanceMemory consolidation, immune repair, metabolic reset, and emotional regulation all depend on it.
Chronic inflammation is the common threadPoor diet, poor sleep, and stress all drive inflammation. Each pillar matters because each dials it up or down.
Purpose and community extend healthspanSocial isolation is as harmful as smoking 15 cigarettes/day. Meaning reduces cortisol, reduces inflammation, improves adherence to everything else.
