Why superelf 为什么选 superelf

  • Your data is stored on your machine Each person's conversations, memory, notes and apps are files in their own folder on your laptop or VM. Whenever the AI works for you (chat, apps, summaries, research, scheduled briefings), the content it needs is sent to the models and search services you set up, which may be cloud services or run on your own machine. Scheduled jobs that fetch outside data, such as a pollen forecast, send what that lookup needs (for example a ZIP code) to that service, and briefing emails, if you turn them on, go out through an email service. On a Mac, an encrypted nightly backup can go where you choose. 数据存在你的机器上：每个人的对话、记忆、笔记和应用都是你自己机器上独立文件夹里的文件。每当 AI 为你工作（对话、应用、总结、研究、定时简报），所需内容会发给你设置的模型和搜索服务，它们可能在云端，也可能在你自己的机器上。获取外部数据的定时任务（如花粉预报）会把查询所需的信息（如邮编）发给该服务；开启邮件简报时，简报经邮件服务发出。在 Mac 上可设置每晚加密备份到你指定的地方。
  • Apps built by asking Say "make an app that tracks my glucose" and it appears on your home screen. Included: Bible (BSB and the Chinese Union Version), a handwriting Journal with Apple Pencil vector ink, Tasks, Diabetes, Longevity. 说一句，就有一个应用。自带：圣经（BSB 与和合本）、Apple Pencil 手写日记、待办、血糖、长寿健康。
  • Every app is sandboxed Generated apps are blocked from fetch, XHR and WebSockets, and hold no token of their own. An app reads, saves and asks only through the shell, which checks every request. 应用沙箱：AI 生成的应用被禁止使用 fetch、XHR 和 WebSocket，也没有自己的令牌，读写和请求都只能经由主程序，并由它逐一检查。
  • Private access Reach it from anywhere over your own Tailscale network, with HTTPS, instead of opening it to the internet. 私密访问：通过你自己的 Tailscale 网络（HTTPS）随处访问，不必向公网开放。
  • Works offline Once a device has saved them for offline use (it does this in the background while online), the Journal and Bible apps, and the note text, ink and chapters it has kept, keep working without a connection (photos in notes still need it). Saves made offline sync when you are back, and a clashing notebook page keeps both versions. AI features need the node. 离线可用：设备联网时会在后台保存离线副本，保存完成后，日记和圣经应用，以及它留存的笔记文字、手写笔迹和经文章节，断网也能用（笔记里的照片仍需联网）；离线修改联网后自动同步，笔记页冲突时两份都保留。AI 功能需要连上主机。
  • For the whole family Separate logins and workspaces for each member, age-appropriate personas for kids and teens, and an audit log for the owner. 全家可用：每人独立账号与空间，孩子和青少年有合适的角色设定，管理者可查审计日志。
  • Simple to run Plain Node 22 with no npm packages, one install script for macOS, Linux or Windows. After you update the node, open phones and iPads load the new version, or offer a one-tap reload while you are busy. 简单：纯 Node 22，不依赖任何 npm 包，macOS / Linux / Windows 一个安装脚本；主机更新后，手机和 iPad 会自动载入新版；正在使用时则提示一键刷新。

What you need 你需要什么

  • Your own Claude Code sign-in: chat with Claude and app building run through it. GPT through Codex, or other models, are optional. 你自己的 Claude Code 账号：与 Claude 对话和生成应用都通过它运行；经 Codex 的 GPT 或其他模型可选。
  • An always-on computer: a laptop, a Linux VM or a small server. 一台常开的电脑：笔记本、Linux 虚拟机或小服务器。
  • Tailscale on that computer and your phones, signed in to one tailnet, for private access from anywhere. 在这台电脑和手机上安装 Tailscale 并登录同一个 tailnet，以便随处私密访问。
