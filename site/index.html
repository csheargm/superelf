Where your data goes你的数据去哪里

Self-hosted, your files stay on your machine; what the AI works on goes to the models you choose.自己部署时，文件留在你的机器上；AI 处理的内容会发给你选择的模型。

What leaves your machine哪些内容会离开你的机器
  • Content for chat, apps and research goes to the models and search services you set up, in the cloud or local.对话、应用和研究所需内容会发给你设置的模型和搜索服务，云端或本地。
  • Scheduled lookups send only what they need, such as a ZIP code for a pollen forecast.定时查询只发送所需信息，例如花粉预报用的邮编。
  • Briefing emails, if you turn them on, go out through an email service.开启邮件简报时，简报经邮件服务发出。
  • On a Mac, an optional encrypted nightly backup goes where you choose.在 Mac 上可选每晚加密备份到你指定的地方。
